# Evidence standard
This is a proposed CAGEBREAK workflow, not an empirical finding.

For each run, record the exact model identifier and version, date, system instructions, complete conversation, sampling settings, attack budget, baseline, evaluator rubric, and failures. Separate source-reported results from locally reproduced results.

# Interpretation
Do not infer model-wide safety from one prompt. Distinguish refusal bypass, task failure, instruction conflict, and apparent persona shifts. Keep contradictory observations and evaluation limitations.
